# Review Open Loops

Find unfinished obligations and explain how each can move forward or be deliberately resolved.

## Gather a bounded view

Read [the shared work-record contract](../checkpoint/references/work-records.md). Inspect the scoped work index, recent log, systems map, and relevant task, handoff, or decision sources. Use existing registered project links for a requested broader review. State checked scope, time, and missing sources.

Without records, use available context and mark the review provisional. Absence of evidence is not proof that every obligation is closed. Do not search unrelated communications or accounts to claim comprehensive coverage.

## Identify actionable gaps

Look for unresolved questions and decisions, commitments with no next action or owner, waiting items whose follow-up trigger has arrived, conflicting status, possible duplicates, and work reported finished without enough evidence to resolve it.

Use agreed dates or review cadences to call something overdue. Otherwise report time since the last observation and why a check may be useful; age alone does not establish urgency or abandonment. Preserve deliberate deferrals whose triggers have not occurred. Separate agent suggestions from adopted work.

For each meaningful finding, show the existing ID or source link, evidence, why it matters, and a recommended disposition:

- Act or clarify the next action.
- Decide a specific unresolved choice.
- Follow up with the known owner or verify a source.
- Defer with a reason and review trigger.
- Close or cancel when supported by evidence or the user's decision.

Distinguish a recommendation from an applied change. If duplicate identity is uncertain, expose the possible overlap instead of merging away details.

## Resolve within the request

A review is read-only by default. If the user also requests cleanup, apply supported local record corrections through [checkpoint](../checkpoint/SKILL.md), preserving stable IDs, history, and source authority. Routine evidence-backed updates need no extra confirmation. “Clean up” does not authorize inventing cancellations, deadlines, or commitments; leave decision-bearing items pending and state the choice needed.

Follow-up recommendations do not send messages or create reminders. External updates require authorization for that action, and a local note cannot close an authoritative external task.

Lead with the most consequential few findings and the first useful action; make a full inventory available when requested. End by stating what was reviewed, what changed if anything, and what still requires a decision. Do not create a second backlog just to store the review.
